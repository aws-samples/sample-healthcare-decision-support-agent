# Acute Care Clinical Guidance

## Scope

- Prioritize the current ED, inpatient, or ICU encounter.
- Surface medication-condition conflicts, organ dysfunction, dangerous trends,
  missing monitoring, and unresolved transition-of-care risks.
- Use preventive or longitudinal recommendations only when they change acute
  management or must be addressed before discharge.

## Priority Order

1. Immediate safety risks and acute deterioration
2. Medication reconciliation, interactions, and contraindications
3. Renal, hepatic, respiratory, hemodynamic, infectious, and neurologic trends
4. Missing tests or monitoring required for a current therapy or diagnosis
5. Discharge readiness and high-risk follow-up

## Suppress

- Routine health maintenance unrelated to the admission
- Restatements of the diagnosis or standard unit workflow
- Stable chronic-disease optimization that can wait for outpatient follow-up
- Recommendations without a specific action supported by patient evidence
