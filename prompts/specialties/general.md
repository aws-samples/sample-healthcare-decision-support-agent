# General Practice Clinical Guidance

## Focus Areas
- Preventive care and screening
- Chronic disease management
- Medication reconciliation
- Lifestyle interventions

## Key Conditions to Prioritize
- Hypertension
- Diabetes (Type 2)
- Hyperlipidemia
- Obesity
- Depression/Anxiety
- Osteoporosis screening

## Clinical Guidelines Emphasis
- USPSTF (United States Preventive Services Task Force) screening recommendations
- CDC vaccination schedules
- HEDIS quality measures
- Evidence-based preventive care guidelines

## Nudge Generation Priorities
1. Overdue preventive screenings (colorectal, breast, cervical cancer)
2. Vaccination status (flu, pneumonia, shingles, COVID-19)
3. Chronic disease control (A1c, BP, lipids)
4. Medication adherence gaps
5. Lifestyle counseling opportunities (smoking cessation, weight management)

## Special Considerations
- Focus on whole-patient assessment across multiple systems
- Balance acute visit needs with chronic disease management
- Emphasize preventive interventions with strong evidence base
- Consider social determinants of health

## Category Mapping for General Practice

### gaps_in_care
- Cancer screenings (colorectal, breast, cervical, lung)
- Vaccination updates (flu, pneumonia, shingles, COVID-19, Tdap)
- SDOH screening (food insecurity, housing, transportation)
- Annual wellness visit components
- Depression/anxiety screening (PHQ-9, GAD-7)

### treatment_recommendations
- Medication reconciliation discrepancies
- Statin therapy for cardiovascular risk
- Antihypertensive optimization
- Smoking cessation pharmacotherapy
- Weight management interventions

### risk_alerts
- Fall risk in elderly
- Polypharmacy concerns (≥5 medications)
- Drug-drug interactions
- Acute clinical deterioration indicators

### follow_up_actions
- Abnormal screening results requiring follow-up
- Specialist referral coordination
- Lab result review and patient notification
- Post-hospitalization follow-up

### community_data_integration
- External provider visit data
- Community health exchange information
- External lab or imaging results
- Emergency department visit alerts

### clinical_monitoring
- Chronic disease metrics (BP, A1c, weight trends)
- Medication adherence tracking
- Symptom progression monitoring

### operational_efficiency
- Care gap closure opportunities
- Quality measure documentation
- Preventive care scheduling
