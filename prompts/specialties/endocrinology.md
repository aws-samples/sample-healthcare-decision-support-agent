# Endocrinology Clinical Guidance

## Focus Areas
- Diabetes management and complications prevention
- Thyroid disorders
- Metabolic syndrome
- Bone health and osteoporosis
- Obesity and weight management

## Key Conditions to Prioritize
- Diabetes (Type 1 and Type 2) - glycemic control, complications screening
- Diabetic complications (retinopathy, nephropathy, neuropathy)
- Thyroid dysfunction (hypo/hyperthyroidism, nodules)
- Metabolic syndrome and prediabetes
- Osteoporosis and fracture risk
- Adrenal and pituitary disorders

## Clinical Guidelines Emphasis
- ADA Standards of Care (diabetes management)
- Endocrine Society clinical practice guidelines
- AACE/ACE diabetes treatment algorithms
- Thyroid disease management guidelines
- Bone health and osteoporosis prevention (NOF, ISCD)

## Nudge Generation Priorities
1. Diabetes control optimization (A1c targets, medication intensification)
2. Diabetes complications screening (annual eye exam, urine albumin, foot exam)
3. Cardiovascular risk reduction in diabetes (statin, ACE-i, aspirin)
4. Continuous glucose monitoring consideration for insulin users
5. Thyroid function monitoring (TSH for hypo/hyperthyroidism)
6. DEXA scan for osteoporosis screening
7. Weight management and metabolic interventions

## Special Considerations
- Prioritize A1c control while avoiding hypoglycemia
- Screen for microvascular and macrovascular complications annually
- Consider newer diabetes agents (GLP-1 RA, SGLT2i) for cardiovascular/renal benefits
- Monitor thyroid function in patients on levothyroxine
- Assess bone health in high-risk populations (postmenopausal women, steroid use)
- Evaluate for metabolic syndrome components

## Category Mapping for Endocrinology

### gaps_in_care
- Annual diabetic eye exam overdue
- Foot exam not documented
- Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) screening
- DEXA scan for osteoporosis screening
- TSH monitoring in hypothyroidism

### treatment_recommendations
- GLP-1 receptor agonist consideration for T2DM + obesity
- SGLT2 inhibitor for cardiovascular/renal protection
- Insulin initiation or intensification
- Thyroid medication dose adjustment
- Bisphosphonate for osteoporosis

### risk_alerts
- Hypoglycemia risk (frequent lows on CGM, A1c < 6.5% on insulin)
- Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) indicators
- Hyperosmolar hyperglycemic state (HHS) risk
- Thyroid storm indicators
- Critical glucose values

### follow_up_actions
- Abnormal A1c requiring plan adjustment
- Elevated UACR requiring ACE-i/ARB
- Abnormal TSH requiring medication change
- Osteoporosis diagnosis requiring treatment initiation

### clinical_monitoring
- Blood glucose trends
- CGM glucose variability
- Weight changes in obesity management
- Thyroid function stability

### operational_efficiency
- Diabetes education completion
- Transition from inpatient to outpatient insulin regimen
- Endocrine consult follow-up arrangements
