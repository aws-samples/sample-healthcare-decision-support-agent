# Cardiology Clinical Guidance

## Focus Areas
- Cardiovascular risk assessment and prevention
- Heart failure management
- Arrhythmia detection and management
- Coronary artery disease optimization
- Lipid management

## Key Conditions to Prioritize
- Atrial fibrillation (anticoagulation, rate/rhythm control)
- Heart failure (GDMT optimization, volume status)
- Coronary artery disease (antiplatelet therapy, lipid control)
- Hypertension (target BP goals, resistant HTN)
- Valvular heart disease (surveillance, intervention timing)

## Clinical Guidelines Emphasis
- ACC/AHA cardiovascular disease prevention guidelines
- Heart Failure Society guidelines (GDMT pillars)
- Atrial fibrillation stroke prevention (CHA2DS2-VASc, HAS-BLED)
- Lipid management guidelines (statin intensity, PCSK9 inhibitors)
- Anticoagulation and antiplatelet therapy recommendations

## Nudge Generation Priorities
1. GDMT optimization for heart failure (ACE-i/ARB, beta-blocker, MRA, SGLT2i)
2. Anticoagulation for atrial fibrillation with elevated stroke risk
3. Lipid control (statin therapy, LDL targets)
4. Blood pressure optimization to guideline-directed targets
5. Cardiac device evaluation (ICD, CRT eligibility)

## Special Considerations
- Evaluate for guideline-directed medical therapy gaps
- Consider cardiovascular risk calculators (ASCVD, HF risk scores)
- Balance multiple medication classes with patient tolerance
- Monitor for medication interactions (e.g., anticoagulation + antiplatelet)
- Assess functional capacity and symptoms progression

## Category Mapping for Cardiology

### gaps_in_care
- Lipid panel screening gaps
- ECG/echocardiogram follow-up gaps
- Cardiac rehab referrals
- Coronary calcium scoring eligibility

### treatment_recommendations
- GDMT optimization (ACE-i/ARB, beta-blocker, MRA, SGLT2i)
- Anticoagulation for AFib (CHA2DS2-VASc ≥ 2)
- Statin therapy initiation/intensification
- PCSK9 inhibitor consideration for high-risk patients

### risk_alerts
- STEMI indicators (troponin elevation, ECG changes)
- Decompensated heart failure (weight gain, BNP elevation)
- Critical troponin elevations
- New arrhythmia detection (AFib, VT)

### follow_up_actions
- Abnormal stress test results
- Post-procedure monitoring needs
- Device interrogation requirements
- Elevated BNP requiring follow-up

### clinical_monitoring
- Fluid status changes (daily weights, I/O)
- Volume overload indicators
- Arrhythmia monitoring
- INR monitoring for warfarin

### operational_efficiency
- Discharge readiness for heart failure admissions
- Telemetry discontinuation criteria
- Bridge to outpatient cardiology follow-up
